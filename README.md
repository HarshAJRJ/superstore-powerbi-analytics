# Superstore Sales & Order Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, executive-facing Power BI dashboard built to analyze overall sales performance, regional revenue distribution, customer distribution, and category-level trends using the Superstore Sales dataset.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights. The primary objective is to evaluate overall revenue performance, identify high-value product categories, examine sales trends across multi-year periods, and provide interactive filtering capabilities for executive decision-making.

- **Total Revenue:** $2.26M
- **Total Orders:** 4,922 (Unique)
- **Total Customers:** 793 (Unique)

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
1. **Revenue Drivers:** Which product categories generate the highest proportion of total sales?
2. **Geographic Distribution:** Which geographic regions contribute the most to top-line growth?
3. **Historical Performance:** How have overall sales trajectory and demand volume evolved over time?
4. **Customer Engagement:** What is the distribution of total volume across different customer segments?

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights
* **Top Category:** **Technology** leads total sales at **$0.83M**, outperforming Furniture ($0.73M) and Office Supplies ($0.71M).
* **Regional Dominance:** The **West Region** generated the largest share of revenue at **31.4% ($710.22K)**, followed by the East Region at **29.6% ($669.52K)**.
* **Growth Trajectory:** Yearly sales trends demonstrate steady continuous growth from 2015 through 2018, with consistent upward quarterly trajectories.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Data Pipeline
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI
* **Data Transformation:** Data Cleaning, Type Standardizations, Custom Aggregations (Distinct Count for Order & Customer IDs).
* **Visual Elements:** KPI Banner Cards, Clustered Bar Charts, Time-Series Line Charts, Donut Share Charts, Dropdown Slicers.
